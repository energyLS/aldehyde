Supplementary Fig. \ref{fig:scenario-sel} shows the categorization of the mitigation-export scenarios into different groups, divided into 
pathways of i) quick exports and slow \co mitigation, ii) balanced exports and \co mitigation and iii) slow exports and quick \co mitigation. Supplementary Fig. \ref{fig:scenario-sel-120} shows the categorization for export scenarios up to 120 TWh/a as displayed in Figure \ref{fig:expenses-relative-120}. Supplementary Fig. \ref{fig:scenario-sel-200} extends this categorization taking export quantities up to 200 TWh/a into account, as basis for the sensitivity analysis on high export scenarios shown in Supplementary Fig. \ref{fig:expenses-relative-200}.
