

\bigheading{Supplementary Method 3: Diffusion of Battery Electric Vehicles}
\label{subsec:bev_diffusion}

Figure \ref{fig:bev_diffusion} shows the diffusion of battery electric vehicles depending on the domestic \co mitigation, displaying electric vehicle stock in Morocco. The share rises from 2\% (today's levels) up to a share of 88\% at 100\% domestic \co mitigation in accordance with Rim et al.\cite{Rim2021}.

\begin{figure*}[h]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.7\linewidth]{../graphics_general/policy/bev_share.pdf}
    \caption{Market diffusion of Battery Electricity Vehicles in Morocco. The market diffusion is synthesized based on an s-curve with a growth rate $k=0.2$ and inflection point $x_0=2040$. The share rises from 2\% (today's levels) up to a share of 88\% at 100\% domestic \co mitigation.}
    \label{fig:bev_diffusion}
\end{figure*}
